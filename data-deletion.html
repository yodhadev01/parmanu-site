Data deletion

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Parmanu only connects to accounts owned by its operator, and it stores no data about other people beyond public comments on the channel's own posts.

Remove the app's access

  • Google and YouTube: open Google account permissions, choose the Parmanu app, and select Delete all connections.
  • Instagram: open Instagram Settings → Apps and websites, choose the Parmanu app and select Remove.

Once access is removed, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted from the operator's configuration.

Request deletion of stored data

To have any data related to you deleted (for example a comment you left that the app stored), email rupeelogic.yt@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion" and a link to the comment or account. We delete the data within 30 days and reply to confirm.
