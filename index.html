Parmanu

An internal publishing tool for our own channels.

Parmanu is a private tool used by a single creator to publish original short videos and image carousels to channels they own and operate:

  • Parmanu Finance: personal finance explainers on the YouTube channel Rupee Logic.
  • Parmanu History: history and "what if" stories on the YouTube channel The Alternate Past.

What it does

  • Stores each channel's rendered videos and images in that channel's own Google Drive.
  • Uploads those videos to the channel's own YouTube channel and Instagram account at scheduled times.
  • Reads the channel's own analytics and comments so the creator can review performance and reply.

Uses YouTube API Services

Parmanu uses YouTube API Services to upload each channel's own videos and to read its own statistics, analytics and comments. By using Parmanu you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See our privacy policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

Who can use it

Parmanu is not a public service. There is no sign-up, and it is not offered to third-party users. Each channel's Google and Meta app is used only by that channel's own account.
