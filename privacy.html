Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Parmanu ("the app") is an internal tool that publishes content to YouTube and Instagram channels owned by its operator. It has no public users. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Accounts the app connects to

The app connects only to Google, YouTube and Instagram accounts that belong to the operator's own channels. Each channel is connected through its own Google Cloud app and its own Meta app.

Data the app accesses

  • Google Drive (drive.file): only files and folders the app itself creates, used to store the channel's rendered videos and images. The app cannot see any other files in the Drive.
  • YouTube: uploading the channel's videos, reading the channel's own videos, analytics and comments, and posting replies to comments on the channel's own videos.
  • Instagram: publishing posts to the channel's own professional account, and reading its own insights and comments.
  • Public competitor data: public statistics of other public channels and professional accounts (for example view and follower counts), used to compare performance.

How data is used

Data is used only to publish the channel's own content, to track its performance, and to help the operator reply to comments. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is accessed through YouTube API Services, subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Comments

Comment text left on the channel's own videos and posts may be processed by an AI model to sort comments and draft replies. Drafts are reviewed by the operator before posting. Comment data is not used to train models.

Storage and security

  • Access tokens are stored only in private configuration files on the operator's own computer and server, readable only by the operator.
  • Media files are kept in the channel's own Google Drive. Temporary local copies are deleted automatically after publishing.
  • Publishing records (what was posted, when, and to which channel) are kept in a private database for as long as the channel is operated.

Deleting stored data

Stored tokens are deleted when access is revoked or the channel stops using the app. Data obtained from YouTube API Services is refreshed or deleted within 30 days. To request deletion of any data, see data deletion.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions or from Instagram's Settings → Apps and websites. See data deletion for removing stored data.
